# Axle: AI-Powered Automation

Axle is a no-code platform for building and deploying AI agents to automate your work.

## What is Axle?

Axle allows you to create powerful AI helpers by simply describing what you want. These agents can connect to your existing tools and services, such as GitHub, Google, and X (formerly Twitter), to automate tasks and streamline your workflow. With Axle, you stay in full control, with the ability to set permissions and monitor your agents' actions.

## Key Features

*   **No-Code AI Agent Creation:** Build AI agents in minutes without writing a single line of code.
*   **Seamless Integration:** Connect your favorite apps to give your agents the context they need to perform tasks.
*   **Powerful Automation:** Automate parts of your job and let your AI agents handle the repetitive work.
*   **Full Control:** You define the rules and permissions, ensuring your agents work for you, on your terms.
